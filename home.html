Youth Creative Writing Workshop (Grades 3–5)

Does your child have stories bursting to get out? This 14-week workshop gives young writers the tools, structure, and encouragement to transform their ideas into a complete short story or chapter book.

Students will learn the essential building blocks of storytelling—setting, character, purpose, conflict, action, and creative endings—while exploring topics they choose themselves. With small-group instruction (max 12 students), weekly feedback from both the instructor and peers, and plenty of room for imagination, kids leave with confidence in their writing AND a finished story they created from scratch.

This workshop is for any student who has a story to tell.

Details

  • Grades: 3–5
  • Schedule: Weekly, 1 hour per session
  • Duration: 14 weeks (Spring term, starting mid-March)
  • Fee: FREE
  • Group Size: 12 students maximum
